# Intent
**Problem:** Candidates go quiet on a hiring board, and nobody notices until they accept another offer.
**Tool:** A CLI that reads the board CSV and prints a ranked follow-up list: who, how long they have waited, and what to do next.
**Done means:** it runs on a realistic CSV, ranks late-stage stalls first, survives messy data, and has tests.
**Out of scope:** sending messages, ATS integrations, a UI.
